What it is

ExoMind is a non-invasive, TMS-based therapy: it uses transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS) applied to the scalp. At our clinic it is offered as part of a mental wellness plan alongside our Behavioral Health and Counseling services.

Who it may be for

  • Adults who want to discuss a non-drug, non-invasive option with a provider
  • Patients already working with a counselor or provider who want an added approach
  • People screened and cleared by a provider (some implants and seizure histories rule it out)

What a visit is like

  1. Consultation. A provider reviews your mental health history, current care, and goals.
  2. Your session. You sit comfortably while the device is placed near your head. Session length and series: [confirm]
  3. Ongoing care. Check-ins with your provider and, if you'd like, our counseling team.

Frequently asked questions

Is ExoMind a replacement for therapy or medication?

No. It may be one part of a plan. Don't stop any medication without talking to your provider.

Does it hurt?

Most people feel a tapping sensation on the scalp. Tell staff if you're uncomfortable.

Is it covered by insurance?

Coverage varies. Call us to ask. [confirm]

What if I'm in crisis?

ExoMind is not a crisis service. If you are in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or call 911.

ExoMind is not a cure for depression, anxiety, or any mental health condition. If you are in crisis, call or text 988. This information is educational and is not a promise of results. Outcomes vary from person to person. Talk with a provider about benefits, risks, and alternatives before starting any treatment.
