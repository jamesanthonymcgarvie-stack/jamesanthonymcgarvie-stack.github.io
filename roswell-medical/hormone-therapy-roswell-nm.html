Hormone Therapy in Roswell, NM

Provider-managed hormone therapy in Roswell, NM for men and women, with in-house lab testing, a consultation first, and ongoing monitoring.

What it is

Hormone therapy means using prescribed hormones to address levels a provider has identified as low or out of balance, based on symptoms and lab results. Our in-house lab helps keep testing simple. Therapy types offered (for example, testosterone or menopause care): [confirm]

Who it may be for

  • Women with perimenopause or menopause symptoms
  • Men with symptoms linked to low testosterone
  • Adults who want lab-guided, monitored care from a local provider

What a visit is like

  1. Consultation and labs. We talk through symptoms and health history and order lab work, often in-house.
  2. Your plan. If therapy is appropriate, your provider explains the options, risks, and benefits.
  3. Monitoring. Regular follow-ups and labs help keep your plan safe and on track.

Every plan starts with a consultation. A Roswell Medical Clinic provider reviews your history and decides with you whether Hormone Therapy is appropriate. Pricing and insurance coverage: [confirm]

Frequently asked questions

Do I need lab work first?

Yes. Your provider uses lab results and your symptoms to decide whether hormone therapy is right for you.

Are there risks?

Yes. All hormone therapy carries risks, and they depend on you. Your provider will review them with you.

Is hormone therapy covered by insurance?

Some parts may be covered. Call us to ask. [confirm]

Can I start on a virtual visit?

Virtual visits are available for some appointments. Labs are done in person.

Hormone therapy is prescribed only when a provider decides it's appropriate. This information is educational and is not a promise of results. Outcomes vary from person to person. Talk with a provider about benefits, risks, and alternatives before starting any treatment.
