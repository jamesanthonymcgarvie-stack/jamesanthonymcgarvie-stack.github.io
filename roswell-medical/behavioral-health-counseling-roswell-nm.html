Behavioral Health & Counseling in Roswell, NM

Individual, couples, and child therapy at Roswell Medical Clinic in Roswell, NM. Warm, welcoming therapy rooms, with weekend appointments available.

Book a consultationCall (575) 623-3311

What it is

Talk therapy with our clinic therapist, Elizabeth [confirm], in spaces she designed herself to feel calm and welcoming: the Ember Room, the Spark Room, and a third room (name coming [confirm]). Kids get their own room with paints, beads, and toys, so therapy can feel like play.

Looking for therapeutic technology instead? See ExoMind (TMS) and EMSELLA.

Who it may be for

  • Individuals working through stress, anxiety, grief, or life changes
  • Couples who want to communicate and reconnect
  • Children, in a playful room built just for them
  • Anyone who needs a Saturday or Sunday appointment because weekdays don't work

What a visit is like

  1. Reach out. Call or send a request, and tell us if you need a weekend time.
  2. First session. Get to know your therapist and talk about what you'd like help with, at your pace.
  3. Ongoing care. Regular sessions in a room that feels like yours. We can coordinate with your clinic providers if you'd like.

Every plan starts with a consultation. A Roswell Medical Clinic provider reviews your history and decides with you whether Behavioral Health & Counseling is appropriate. Pricing and insurance coverage: [confirm]

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer weekend appointments?

Yes. Saturday and Sunday counseling appointments are available by appointment for patients who can't come during the week.

Do you see children?

Yes. Our children's room has paints, beads, and toys to help kids feel at ease.

Do you offer couples therapy?

Yes, along with individual and child therapy.

Is counseling covered by insurance?

Coverage varies. Call us to ask. [confirm]

What if I'm in crisis?

Counseling appointments are not a crisis service. If you or someone you love is in crisis, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) or call 911.

If you are in crisis, call or text 988 or call 911 right away. Therapy outcomes vary from person to person.
