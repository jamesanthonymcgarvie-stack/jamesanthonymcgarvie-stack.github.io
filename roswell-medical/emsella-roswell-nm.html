What it is

EMSELLA is a non-invasive chair that uses focused electromagnetic energy to stimulate pelvic floor muscle contractions. You stay fully clothed during the session.

Who it may be for

  • Women and men with bladder leakage or pelvic floor weakness
  • Postpartum patients, once cleared by a provider
  • People who prefer a non-surgical option to discuss with their provider

What a visit is like

  1. Consultation. We review symptoms and screen for things that rule EMSELLA out, such as certain implants or pregnancy.

  3. Back to your day. No downtime is typically needed.

Frequently asked questions

Is EMSELLA painful?

Most people describe it as tingling and strong muscle contractions, not pain. The intensity can be adjusted.

Do I need to undress?

No. You stay fully clothed.

How soon will I notice a difference?

It varies. Some people notice changes during a series and others don't. Your provider will set expectations with you.

Who should not use EMSELLA?

People with certain metal or electronic implants, pregnant patients, and some others. Your provider will screen you.
