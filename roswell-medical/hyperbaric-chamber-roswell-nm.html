What it is

Hyperbaric therapy means resting in a pressurized chamber while breathing concentrated oxygen. Increased pressure allows more oxygen to dissolve into the blood. Chamber type and pressure level: [confirm]

Who it may be for

  • Adults looking for a provider-supervised wellness or recovery option
  • Patients whose provider recommends it as part of a broader care plan
  • People who have been screened and cleared by a provider (some ear, lung, and other conditions may make it unsuitable)

What a visit is like

  1. Consultation. Review of your history, medications, and goals; screening for safety.
  2. Your session. You relax in the chamber, fully clothed, and can usually read or rest. Session length: [confirm]
  3. Follow-up. Your provider reviews how you're doing and whether to continue.

Every plan starts with a consultation. A Roswell Medical Clinic provider reviews your history and decides with you whether Hyperbaric Chamber is appropriate. Pricing and insurance coverage: [confirm]

Frequently asked questions

Does hyperbaric therapy hurt?

Most people feel ear pressure similar to flying, which staff will show you how to clear. Tell staff right away if you feel discomfort.

How many sessions will I need?

That depends on your goals and your provider's recommendation. There is no one-size-fits-all number.

Is it covered by insurance?

Coverage varies and many wellness uses are self-pay. Call us to ask. [confirm]

Can I just walk in for a session?

No. We are appointment-only, and a consultation is required before your first session.

Hyperbaric therapy is not a cure for any disease, and FDA-cleared uses are specific. This information is educational and is not a promise of results. Outcomes vary from person to person. Talk with a provider about benefits, risks, and alternatives before starting any treatment.
